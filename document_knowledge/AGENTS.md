# document_knowledge

## Purpose

Base of the OCA knowledge modules: the Knowledge app menu and the knowledge user and
manager groups that `document_page` and the other modules of the repository build on. It
has no models of its own.

## Notes for 20.0

- Migrated from 19.0 by the open OCA migration pull request; this fork carries it until
  the upstream branch 20.0 has it.
- Odoo 20 has no Community knowledge app: on Community this module and `document_page`
  are the knowledge base; on Enterprise the Odoo Knowledge app exists next to it.
